1. **New SQLite-only constructs from the base work.** `topic GLOB`, `type GLOB`, `NOT INDEXED` and `INDEXED BY` at `events.ts:242-273` landed after both reviews and are exactly what habit 1 forbids. New section 6.7b with a `globPrefix` helper and an empty hint fragment off SQLite; the events index set added to the 7.2 table.

4. **Read snapshot without a lock.** Working through the published-image `CASE` against the serialized-writer invariant shows no fence ordering can surface an unpublished image given a consistent snapshot; the earlier `FOR SHARE` prelude was based on a scenario (two unpublished images of one row) that the writer cannot produce. This is the largest change to the design and it is flagged as argued-not-proven in 16 item 5 with the container test as the arbiter.
5. **Edit-lock read-modify-write** (`edit-lock.ts:141`, `:233`) added to the R14 rule alongside `seq`.
6. **Schema versions** are 14 (boot) and 7 (app) at the working tree, not 13 and 6; `boot-schema.ts:34`/`:88`, `database.ts:20`/`:79`. Adopt-step fixture is now v14.

## What remains an estimate

Everything in section 16 is an estimate or an open decision, and three things in the body are argued rather than measured. The lock-free read snapshot (9.2) is a correctness argument from the writer invariant and the fence cache; it has not been run against a real Postgres or MySQL, and the container test in 12.3 item 2 is what would prove it. The role-membership design in 3.2 was checked against documented Postgres rules (membership confers ownership privileges; `CREATE DATABASE … OWNER` and `ALTER OWNER` require membership; `pg_terminate_backend` allows a member of the signalled role) and against the working tree, not against a running server; Postgres 16's changes to how `INHERIT` is recorded on a grant, and the `pg_database_owner` ownership of `public` on 15+, are the two places a real container could still surprise it, which is why 12.3 item 7 and 16 item 9 exist. The step sizes in section 13 are the investigation's estimates plus the verifier's additions at the same ±40%. The rehearsal-copy budgets, the scratch limit, the remote byte caps and the freeze budget are defaults chosen without measurement, and section 16 says which measurement decides each. Every `packages/` line number is against a working tree that is being rewritten by the base work and will drift before implementation; the constructs they name were verified to exist.
